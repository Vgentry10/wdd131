WDD 131-[.01]

Overview

Purpose

[A personal digital library to catalog and showcase my favorite books of the year, including summaries, ratings, reading notes, and reading progress. The site helps me track favorites, rediscover highlights, and share curated recommendations with friends.]

Audience

[Friends and family, fellow readers, and small reading communities interested in yearly reading highlights and personal recommendations.]

Dynamic elements

[Load books.json via JS and render grid/list of book cards. Client-side filters (genre, status), sort (rating, date, title), and live search with typeahead. Click book card → open accessible modal with full details, editable notes, rating stars, and progress slider. Persist ratings, notes, progress, theme (dark/light), and view preference in localStorage (optionally sync to backend). UI interactions: animated card entrance, lazy load covers, keyboard navigation for modals, and toast notifications for saves.]
